# Privacy Policy: Tema Ops WhatsApp assistant

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy covers the WhatsApp assistant run by Tema, a fast-food chain in Tbilisi, Georgia.
The assistant answers questions from Tema's own staff and management about Tema's sales data,
and can send them the sales report.

## Who can use it

Only phone numbers that Tema has added to an approved list (staff and management).
Messages from any other number are not answered and not processed further.

## What data the assistant handles

When you message the assistant, it receives:

- your WhatsApp phone number and profile name,
- the text of your messages,
- the replies it sends you.

## Why

Only to answer your question or send the report you asked for. The data is not used for
advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone except the services below.

## Services that process the data

| Service | What it does |
|---|---|
| Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) | delivers the messages between you and the assistant |
| Google Cloud (Vertex AI, Gemini) | reads your question and writes the answer |
| LangSmith (LangChain) | records each question, the database query and the answer, so we can find and fix mistakes |
| Google Cloud (our server, Frankfurt) | runs the assistant and holds Tema's business database |

## How long it is kept

- The assistant remembers the last few messages of a conversation, only in memory,
  until the server restarts.
- LangSmith keeps its records for a limited time, normally 14 days.
- Meta keeps WhatsApp messages according to its own policies.

## Your choices

You can stop using the assistant at any time. To ask what data about you is held, or to have
it deleted, write to tema.opsai@gmail.com.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
